## Protocol (shared with the main-phase cells, verified identical by hash)

- 944 original VBench prompts, 5 samples/prompt (official protocol), 16 dimensions.
- Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct rewrites for conditioning; scoring uses the original VBench
  prompt text (`scoring_prompt: original_vbench_prompt`).
- Seed rule: `prompt_index * samples_per_prompt + sample_index`.
- 21 latent / 81 RGB frames, 16 fps, no attention sink, `framewise` method, `ffe` schedule.
- `full_info_sha256=12d720a3…`, `prompt_sha256=d802b483…`, `rewrite_sha256=90dde244…`,
  `manifest_sha256=483f5279…` — identical across all four cells (`full200_ffe`,
  `dmd200_ffe`, `full400_ffe`, `full600_ffe`), verified against each cell's
  `*_paper_protocol_summary.json`. `summarize_paper_aligned_vbench.py` / the driver's own
  `write_comparison` refuse to compare runs whose protocol hashes differ, so this is a
  valid paired comparison against the main-phase baseline.

**Headline pattern:** most quality/consistency dimensions (`subject_consistency`,
`background_consistency`, `temporal_flickering`, `motion_smoothness`, `object_class`,
`multiple_objects`) improve monotonically from step 200 → 400 → 600 — consistent with
the visual-inspection finding below that longer DMD+GAN training keeps improving
structural/subject stability rather than degrading it. But **`dynamic_degree` collapses
monotonically and severely** (1.00 → 0.72 → 0.36): the model is generating steadily
*less* motion as GAN training continues, even though motion that is present stays
smooth and coherent. This single dimension accounts for essentially all of the
semantic-score regression at step 600 (−0.79 total semantic delta vs step 200) and is
why the composite `total_score` is **non-monotonic** — it peaks at step 400 (83.93) and
partially reverts by step 600 (81.24) — rather than continuing to climb with additional
GAN training. `color`, `spatial_relationship`, and `scene` also drift down slightly and
monotonically, a smaller secondary effect.

## Visual inspection (required before relying on the above numbers)

Per the aligned-experiments document's explicit requirement, representative generated
MP4s from `full400_ffe` and `full600_ffe` were inspected at the beginning, middle, and
end of the clip (81 RGB frames @ 16 fps, ≈5.06s; frame 0 / frame 40 / frame 80) against
the `full200_ffe` baseline, before treating the numeric comparison above as valid. The
same six representative prompts used for the main-phase inspection were reused (one,
the beach prompt, pinned to its unambiguous "featuring a steady and smooth perspective"
variant across all three cells, since the bare prompt string is not itself a generated
sample):

| Prompt | full200 (baseline) | full400 (step 400) | full600 (step 600) |
|---|---|---|---|
| "A cat wearing sunglasses at a pool" | Stable, sharp throughout. | Stable, sharp throughout — near-identical to full200 (low-motion prompt). | Stable, sharp throughout — near-identical to full200 (low-motion prompt). |
| "A beautiful coastal beach in spring... (steady and smooth perspective)" | Coherent scene, wave motion at all three timepoints. | Coherent scene, wave motion at all three timepoints. | Coherent scene; waves visibly **calmer/less turbulent** than full200/full400 at mid — consistent with the collapsed `dynamic_degree` score, not a quality defect. |
| "A corgi is playing drum kit." | Coherent corgi + drum kit at begin/mid; camera pans past subject by end. | Sharp, coherent corgi at begin/mid/end — **no transient blur** (dmd200's blur artifact does not appear on this trajectory at any step). | Sharp, coherent corgi at begin/mid/end — same as full400, no blur, no collapse. |
| "A Mars rover moving on Mars" | Coherent rover at begin/mid; end is a fast-pan motion-blur crop (plausible fast camera motion). | Coherent rover at all three timepoints; dust plume kicked up during driving is a plausible dynamic effect, geometry stays sharp throughout. | Coherent, **sharpest geometry of the three** at all three timepoints, but the rover is nearly **stationary with minimal dust** — visibly less dynamic than full200/full400, again consistent with the `dynamic_degree` collapse. |
| "A cute happy Corgi playing in park, sunset" | Corgi partially cropped by camera framing at mid/end but stays a recognizable, coherent shape throughout. | Corgi's identity fully intact and sharp at begin/mid/end (dmd200's full subject-identity collapse on this prompt does not occur on the DMD+GAN trajectory at any step). | Same as full400 — sharp, fully coherent corgi throughout. |
| "A bigfoot walking in the snowstorm." | Coherent creature (rendered wolf-like, a prompt-interpretation choice) at begin/mid; camera pans away by end. | Coherent creature charging at camera, good fur/facial detail retained at all three timepoints, no whiteout. | Coherent creature, arguably the **best fur/lighting detail and sharpest mid-air pose** of the three, no whiteout, no collapse. |

**Assessment:** No collapse was observed in either `full400_ffe` or `full600_ffe` in any
of the 6 sampled prompts across all three timepoints. If anything, subject/structural
fidelity is *better* at step 400 and step 600 than at step 200 in this sample — the two
prompts that showed genuine collapse under `dmd200` (DMD-only) in the main-phase report
(Mars rover, corgi-in-park) show **no collapse at any step** on this DMD+GAN trajectory,
consistent with the per-dimension `subject_consistency`/`multiple_objects`/
`object_class` gains above. The one visible, consistent qualitative change with more
training is **reduced motion/dynamism** — waves calmer, the rover nearly stationary at
step 600 — which is the direct visual counterpart of the `dynamic_degree` collapse
(1.00 → 0.72 → 0.36) documented above. This is being reported as a genuine, expected
training-length trade-off (more GAN steps trade dynamic motion diversity for
structural/quality stability, non-monotonically for the composite score) — not as
evidence that either run's pipeline is broken — and does **not** block relying on the
numeric comparison above; both runs' scores were computed over the full 944-prompt ×
5-sample set, not just these 6 illustrative clips.

Selected frames (extracted via `ffmpeg`, frame 0 / N/2 / N-1) are kept at
`/data/banyuanhao/iclr2027_ckpts/_visual_inspection/` on the local training machine
(outside the repo, outside the published output root) alongside the source `-0.mp4`
files under each cell's `videos/` directory; they are not published as part of this
report. (Frame extraction was run directly on the head node, not via `srun`, since it is
a lightweight, single-frame-per-video CPU operation — `ffmpeg`/`ffprobe` are only
installed on the head node's image, not the compute-node image — and the actual
generation/scoring compute all ran on the compute node, `slinky-14`, via job 8477.)

## Environment notes carried into this run (disclosed, unchanged from the main phase)

- `torch==2.11.0+cu128` / `torchvision==0.26.0` used instead of the pinned
  `2.5.1`/`0.20.1` (no B200/sm_100 kernels in the pinned cu124 build); disclosed and
  checked by `validate_vbench_versions()`.
- `torch.load` default restored to `weights_only=False` for this scoring entry point
  only, to unpickle vbench's vendored, officially-sourced auxiliary-scorer checkpoints
  (not the evaluated model).
- `evaluator.evaluate(..., local=True)` so vbench's CLIP/DINO auxiliary-model loads
  honor the per-job `VBENCH_CACHE_DIR`.
- `detectron2==0.6` built from the official `facebookresearch/detectron2` source,
  pinned to commit `a2f4a8771ab77e8411c26b27f24f9489a28a2453`, for the B200/sm100/cu128
  toolchain — required by VBench's GRiT-based dimensions.
- This job ran `full400_ffe` then `full600_ffe` **sequentially inside one process on
  one exclusive node** (`slinky-14`), so a single shared `VBENCH_CACHE_DIR`
  (`/data/banyuanhao/iclr2027_ckpts/vbench_cache/appendix_full400_full600`) was safe to
  use for both cells (no cross-job cache race, unlike the concurrent main-phase jobs on
  separate nodes) and was pre-seeded from the already-verified `full200_ffe` cache to
  avoid redundant downloads.
