## Protocol (shared across both cells, verified identical by hash)

- 944 original VBench prompts, 5 samples/prompt (official protocol), 16 dimensions.
- Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct rewrites for conditioning; scoring uses the
  original VBench prompt text (`scoring_prompt: original_vbench_prompt`).
- Seed rule: `prompt_index * samples_per_prompt + sample_index`.
- 21 latent / 81 RGB frames, 16 fps, no attention sink, `framewise` method, `ffe` schedule.
- `full_info_sha256=12d720a3…`, `prompt_sha256=d802b483…`,
  `rewrite_sha256=90dde244…`, `manifest_sha256=483f5279…` — identical for
  `full200_ffe` and `dmd200_ffe` (verified by
  `summarize_paper_aligned_vbench.py`, which refuses to compare runs whose
  protocol hashes differ).

## Visual inspection (required before relying on the above numbers)

Per the aligned-experiments document's explicit requirement, representative
generated MP4s from **both sides of the pair** were inspected at the
beginning, middle, and end of the clip (81 RGB frames @ 16 fps, ≈5.06s;
frame 0 / frame 40 / frame 80) before treating the numeric comparison as
valid. Six prompts were selected to span static/dynamic content, style
transfer, animal/human-like action, and fast camera or subject motion:

| Prompt | full200 (DMD+GAN) | dmd200 (DMD-only) |
|---|---|---|
| "A cat wearing sunglasses at a pool" | Stable throughout; sharp subject at begin/mid/end. | Stable throughout; sharp subject at begin/mid/end. Near-identical quality — low-motion prompt. |
| "A beautiful coastal beach in spring, waves lapping on sand..." | Coherent scene and wave motion at all three timepoints. | Coherent scene and wave motion at all three timepoints. Near-identical — low subject-motion prompt. |
| "A corgi is playing drum kit." | Coherent corgi + drum kit at begin/mid; camera pans past subject by end (expected framing change, not collapse). | Sharp at begin; **heavy motion blur / texture smear on the corgi's body at mid** (paw/arm motion), but **recovers to a sharp, coherent face by end**. Transient blur, not a lasting collapse. |
| "A Mars rover moving on Mars" | Coherent rover at begin/mid; end frame is a fast-pan motion-blur crop (mostly one wheel + rock formation) — plausible fast camera motion, not structural collapse. | Rover coherent at begin; **by mid, the wheels/undercarriage dissolve into an indistinct fuzzy blob and the geometry does not recover by end** — a genuine structural/texture collapse under fast motion. |
| "A cute happy Corgi playing in park, sunset" | Corgi partially cropped by camera framing at mid/end but remains a recognizable, coherent shape throughout. | Corgi's identity is **fully lost by mid** (only an indistinct white blur remains) and by end the frame shows an **unrelated bright yellow/orange blob (looks like a ball) with a smeared, flame-like artifact** where the subject should be — full subject-identity collapse, not just motion blur. |
| "A bigfoot walking in the snowstorm." | Coherent creature (rendered wolf-like, a prompt-interpretation choice, not a defect) at begin/mid; camera pans past it to a clean snowy-forest scene by end. | Coherent at begin; by mid the frame is dominated by snowstorm particles occluding the subject (plausible); **by end the frame has gone into an overexposed, textureless whiteout** with only faint tree/hill silhouettes surviving — a clear (if borderline "diegetic," i.e. explainable as an intensifying blizzard) quality collapse. |

**Assessment:** No collapse was observed on the **full200 (DMD+GAN)** side in
any of the 6 sampled prompts across all three timepoints — camera-motion
crops and framing changes were the only artifacts, all plausible outputs of
genuine fast subject/camera motion. The **dmd200 (DMD-only)** side showed
clear structural/subject-identity collapse by the middle or end of the clip
in 3 of 6 sampled prompts (Mars rover, corgi-in-park, bigfoot-in-snowstorm) —
all three are the more dynamic/fast-motion prompts in the sample — while the
two low-motion prompts (cat-at-pool, beach) and the corgi-drumming prompt
(transient blur only, recovers) showed no lasting collapse. This is
consistent with, and gives qualitative support to, the quantitative GAN gain
above (particularly the `subject_consistency`, `multiple_objects`, and
`spatial_relationship` gaps): the DMD-only model degrades under sustained
fast motion in a way the DMD+GAN model does not, in this sample. This is
being reported as a genuine, expected ablation finding — not as evidence
that either run's pipeline is broken — and does **not** block relying on the
numeric comparison above; both runs' scores were computed over the full
944-prompt × 5-sample set, not just these 6 illustrative clips.

Selected frames (extracted via `ffmpeg`, frame 0 / N/2 / N-1) are kept at
`/data/banyuanhao/iclr2027_ckpts/_visual_inspection/` on the local training
machine (outside the repo, outside the published output root) alongside the
source `-0.mp4` files under each cell's `videos/` directory; they are not
published as part of this report.

## Environment notes carried into this run (disclosed, unchanged from prior rounds)

- `torch==2.11.0+cu128` / `torchvision==0.26.0` used instead of the pinned
  `2.5.1`/`0.20.1` (no B200/sm_100 kernels in the pinned cu124 build);
  disclosed and checked by `validate_vbench_versions()`.
- `torch.load` default restored to `weights_only=False` for this scoring
  entry point only, to unpickle vbench's vendored, officially-sourced
  auxiliary-scorer checkpoints (not the evaluated model).
- `evaluator.evaluate(..., local=True)` so vbench's CLIP/DINO auxiliary-model
  loads honor the per-job `VBENCH_CACHE_DIR` instead of a shared home-directory
  cache (avoids the concurrent-job cache race that caused earlier failures).
- `detectron2==0.6` built from the official `facebookresearch/detectron2`
  source, pinned to commit `a2f4a8771ab77e8411c26b27f24f9489a28a2453`, for
  the B200/sm100/cu128 toolchain — required by VBench's GRiT-based dimensions
  (`object_class`, `multiple_objects`, `color`, `spatial_relationship`), not
  present in `requirements-vbench.txt`. Built via a scoped, user-approved
  sbatch job (see `logs/iclr2027_submit/build_detectron2.slurm`); this
  mirrors a prior disclosed gap from the earlier H200-cluster evaluation
  round (see `RUN_REPORT.md` §8.4), rebuilt here for the new hardware/CUDA
  combination.
